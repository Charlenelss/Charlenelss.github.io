morning-brief

A personal, self-hosted tool that sends its owner a weekday morning summary in Telegram: calendar, emails needing a reply, weather and a market note. It is used by one person only and is not offered to the public.

Privacy policy

  • Data accessed: the owner's Google Calendar events (read-only) and Gmail messages (read-only), plus permission to create Gmail drafts. The app never sends email.
  • Where it is processed: only on the owner's own computer, including a locally run language model. Nothing is sold, shared or sent to third parties, except that the summary itself is delivered to the owner's private Telegram chat.
  • Storage: an OAuth token and a small list of snoozed reminders are kept on the owner's computer. No email content is stored.
  • Revoking access: remove "morning-brief" at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Terms
